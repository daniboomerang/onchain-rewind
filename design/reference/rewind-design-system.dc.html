Buttons are 44px tall to give a comfortable click and tap target. Pressing scales them to .97 over 80ms. The focus ring is primary with a 2px background-colored gap, shown only for keyboard focus. Primary button text is dark in dark mode because white on #00a3f5 fails contrast; it's white in light mode.
