S06Frame C: burst into card 1

Burst lasts 700ms. The ring breaks apart and particles fly outward (ease-out) while fading. Card 1's elements start entering 200ms in; the frame shows about 400ms. The chrome fades in with them.
ParticlesCount: one per transaction, capped at 1,500 (desktop) or 600 (mobile). Below 240 transactions, dim "dust" particles (fg at 25% opacity) are added to reach 240 total.
Size: radius 0.8–2.2px, scaled for screen pixel density.
Color: 88% fg (#fff) at 30–90% opacity, 9% primary (#00a3f5), 3% notice (#ff9d1c).
Gather shapeA ring, read as a rewind loop, centered 10px above the viewport center. Radius 200px (desktop) or 120px (mobile), band ±14px (Gaussian). It rotates at 6°/s. Each particle eases to its slot on the ring over 900–1400ms (ease-in-out), leaving a 6px trail.
TimingScatter: 0–600ms.
Gather: waits for the data, at least 3,200ms total. After 12s the API error screen shows (S13).
Hold: 400ms, with the counter reading "1,284 transactions".
Burst: 700ms, ease-out.
Card 1: enters 200ms into the burst.
CounterBefore the total is known: "Reading transactions…" while the number in the ring counts up page by page (240ms per step, ease-out). Once the total is known: "Reading 1,284 transactions…". On completion: "Done" for 400ms, then the burst. Uses aria-live="polite", updating at most once per second.
Reduced motionNo canvas. A centered static line reads "Reading 1,284 transactions…", with the number changing directly (no count-up). When loading finishes, the line crossfades into card 1 over 240ms. Cards crossfade without sliding, bars and the chart appear already complete, and numbers show their final value.

Pausing (hold the pointer 200ms or more, or press Space) changes: the current segment's fill stops and drops to 50% opacity, with a pause glyph after the segments. In-flight count-ups, bar growth and line drawing freeze exactly where they are (shown mid-count). Content dims to 85% opacity over 150ms, and a pause pill replaces the navigation hint. Everything else stays in place. Releasing resumes from the same point; a hold is never counted as a navigation click.
